make selection:
- Select: Iterative and Incremental combined with Agile (Hybrid Approach)

Justification:
We choose a hybrid approach that combines Agile with Iterative and Incremental development.
First, we want to adapt our design as we learn more about user needs, and we need to test the system on different platforms (Windows, macOS, Linux) during the development process, not just at the end. Agile is very suitable because it values change and supports continuous testing.
Second, this approach is not strictly Agile because we do not have real outside users; we (the developers) act as the users (proxy users). 
Therefore, we plan two clear iterations: Iteration 1 builds and tests the core security engine, and Iteration 2 adds the GUI and improves cross-platform compatibility. This gives us both a clear step-by-step plan and the flexibility of Agile.
